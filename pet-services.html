How to Choose the Right Dog Service

  • Check Reviews: Look for recent, positive reviews from other pet owners.
  • Ask About Experience: Choose providers with experience in your dog’s breed, age, or special needs.
  • Meet & Greet: Arrange a meeting before booking to ensure a good fit for your dog’s personality.
  • Verify Insurance: Make sure sitters, walkers, and daycares are insured and background-checked.
  • Emergency Plan: Ask about emergency procedures and vet access.
