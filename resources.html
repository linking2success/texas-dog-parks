The Complete Dog Park Checklist

Before heading to the dog park, make sure you have everything you need for a safe and enjoyable visit. Our comprehensive checklist covers all the essentials.

✅ Current vaccinations and health records
✅ Proper identification tags
✅ Leash and collar
✅ Water and bowl
✅ Waste bags
✅ Treats for training
✅ First aid kit
✅ Emergency contact numbers
