Dog Park Visit Flowchart

flowchart TD
    A[ Arrive at Dog Park] --> B{ Dog Current on Vaccines?}
    B -->|No| C[ Visit Vet First]
    B -->|Yes| D[ Keep Dog Leashed to Entrance]
    D --> E[ Enter Through Double Gates]
    E --> F[ Remove Leash Immediately]
    F --> G[ Maintain Constant Supervision]
    G --> H{ Observe Dog Interactions}
    H -->|Positive Play| I[ Continue Monitoring]
    H -->|Signs of Stress/Aggression| J[ Intervene Immediately]
    J --> K[ Remove Dog from Situation]
    I --> L{ Need to Leave?}
    L -->|No| G
    L -->|Yes| M[ Leash Dog at Exit]
    M --> N[ Clean Up Any Waste]
    N --> O[ Exit Through Double Gates]
    K --> M
    C --> P[ Return When Healthy]
